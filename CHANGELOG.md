## 1.0.0

- First open-source release under the name **darth** (previously "Booth Mixer").
- Two decks with 3-band isolator, kills, filter and crossfader.
- Echo, reverb and flanger send FX with tap tempo.
- Ten punch-in FX pads and the Smash master compressor.
- keinedelay tape delay with 12 s looper.
- Live input and output device routing (BlackHole).
