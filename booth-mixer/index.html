How to route Spotify

Spotify in Chrome. Play it at open.spotify.com, then press Grab tab audio. The tab goes quiet and you hear it only through the mixer.

Spotify app, or any sound. Install the free BlackHole 2ch driver and set your Mac's sound output to it. Pick BlackHole as In and your speakers or headphones as Out, then press Use input.
